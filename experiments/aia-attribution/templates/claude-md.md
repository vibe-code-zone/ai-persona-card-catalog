- `CONTENT_TYPE_HERE` - Type of AI-generated content
- `HUMAN_ROLE_HERE` - Human's role in the process
- `REVIEW_STATUS_HERE` - Review and approval status
- `PROJECT_INSTRUCTIONS_HERE` - Persona-specific instructions
